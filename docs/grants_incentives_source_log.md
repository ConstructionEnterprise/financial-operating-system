# Grants & Incentives Source Log

This source log records initial discovery sources as of **August 21, 2026**. Records in the future program universe will remain research-only until CE/FF explicitly qualifies them; a program listing does not establish CE eligibility, a live application window, an award amount, or an approval.

The [Texas Economic Development & Tourism incentive and financial programs page](https://gov.texas.gov/business/page/incentives) describes Texas programs across performance-based incentives, job-training partnerships, research, lending, financing, and tax programs. The [Texas Workforce Commission Skills Development Fund page](https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/skills-development-fund) describes a workforce training pathway that is applied for by specific eligible applicant types in partnership with businesses. The [NSF SBIR Advanced Manufacturing topic page](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/topics/advanced-manufacturing/) describes an R&D funding topic and its stated applicant conditions. [Manufacturing.gov](https://www.manufacturing.gov/) describes itself as a federal manufacturing-program and funding-opportunity hub.

The current seed scope includes only programs whose official pages are recorded. Every deadline, current-open status, award ceiling, match percentage, and eligibility determination remains **missing** unless independently captured from the program’s primary documentation and dated in the corresponding record.

## Official discovery surfaces

The [Grants.gov home page](https://www.grants.gov/) describes its search as a way to discover federal opportunities and notes that published federal opportunities are for organizations and entities supporting government-funded programs and projects. The [Simpler.Grants.gov search](https://simpler.grants.gov/search) provides an official search surface that displays opportunity status, title, agency, close date, and award minimum/maximum when reported. Its results are discovery inputs only: CE/FF will not interpret an agency listing as evidence of CE eligibility, Texas applicability, a current application window, or a decision-ready award amount without opening and recording the underlying official opportunity documentation.

Additional research leads requiring primary-page verification include [Austin Energy commercial rebates](https://austinenergy.com/energy-efficiency/rebates-incentives/commercial), [Taylor Economic Development incentives](https://tayloredc.org/site-selectors/incentives), [La Marque developmental incentives](https://www.cityoflamarque.gov/190/Developmental-Incentives), and the [Texas Comptroller’s Inflation Reduction Act programs page](https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/seco/funding/ira/). These sources are not yet program records and must not be used to populate availability, award, deadline, or eligibility fields until reviewed at the program level.

New official commercial-energy research leads include [Oncor Basic Commercial](https://www.oncor.com/content/oncorwww/talot/en/home/get-started/business/basic-commercial.html), [Oncor Commercial Provider](https://www.oncor.com/content/oncorwww/us/en/home/partners/eepm-home/commercial-provider.html), and [CenterPoint electric efficiency business programs](https://www.centerpointenergy.com/en-us/business/save-energy-money/electric-efficiency-programs/business-efficiency-programs?sa=ho). They are preserved as discovery sources only until their current jurisdiction, applicant pathway, equipment eligibility, and program status are captured from the applicable official terms.

On August 21, 2026, primary-page review confirmed the named commercial program families added to the research seed. Oncor’s Basic Commercial page identifies an Oncor-service-area route for lighting, heating/cooling, and commercial-kitchen improvements and states that the program uses participating providers. Oncor’s Commercial Provider page identifies Commercial Standard Offer, Load Management, and Solar Photovoltaic program pathways. CenterPoint’s Houston business-efficiency page identifies CHEF, Load Management, A/C Tune-up, Data Center Energy Efficiency, Healthcare Energy Efficiency, Retro-Commissioning, Schools and Non-Profit Efficiency, and Electric Equipment Incentives. The seed still marks every one as **research** with application window, CE eligibility, project eligibility, award amount, and current availability unset until CE/FF examines the applicable official program details.

Additional primary-page review verified CPS Energy’s commercial Sustainable Tomorrow Energy Plan and Commercial Demand Response references; the City of Dallas program families for business development, community development loans/grants, real-estate incentives, and small-business assistance; and the City of Houston’s Economic Development Grants process. Dallas describes several program families as incentive routes and Houston describes a negotiated economic-development process; neither page establishes CE/FF qualification. These records remain research-stage inputs, not applications or awards.

Further official regional research leads include [Texas City EDC local and state incentives](https://www.texascityedc.com/166/Local-State-Incentives), [Plano incentives](https://www.planotexas.org/incentives), [Port Arthur EDC incentives](https://portarthuredc.org/incentives/tax-incentives/), and [Port Arthur municipal economic-development incentives](https://www.portarthurtx.gov/585/Economic-Development-Incentives). These are discovery references pending program-level verification.

Federal primary-source discovery references for further records include the [DOE AMMTO funding-opportunities page](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ammto/ammto-funding-opportunities), [DOE MESC funding page](https://www.energy.gov/mesc/funding), [Commerce grants and contract opportunities](https://www.commerce.gov/work-with-us/grants-and-contract-opportunities), and [NSF SBIR/STTR](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/small-business-innovation-research-small-business-technology). These are program and funding-discovery surfaces; no current opportunity status, award size, or CE/FF eligibility is assumed from the directory listing alone.

Primary-page review confirmed NSF SBIR/STTR Phase I, Phase II, Fast-Track, and Supplements as named program routes; Commerce’s links to NIST advanced-manufacturing NOFOs, EDA funding, MBDA Grants, NTIA telecommunications grants, and the Market Development Cooperator Program; and DOE AMMTO’s manufacturing R&D funding-opportunity discovery surface. NSF’s page includes current dates and award information, but the research seed intentionally keeps dates and award fields unset pending opportunity-specific CE review. DOE’s AMMTO page explicitly notes that displayed amounts and schedules are subject to change; these entries therefore remain research records rather than current application instructions.

The final group of the initial seed uses named program families drawn from those same DOE, NSF, Commerce, and Texas local-government pages. In particular, AMMTO’s page lists the named manufacturing initiatives, but also contains archived or time-sensitive solicitation information. Accordingly, every AMMTO-derived record remains **research** with `applicationWindow = unknown`; it is deliberately not represented as open, funded, or CE-eligible. This expands the initial source-attributed universe to 100 records without fabricating live funding status.
