# Capital Paths

The Financial Operating System presents one aggregate capital plan through four distinct capital paths. The distinction matters because each path has a different counterparty, diligence process, capital instrument, and risk boundary.

## Equity Investors

Equity supports the technology company and operating platform. The workspace covers investor records, thesis fit, check size, geography, relationship status, outreach history, campaigns, approval-first email, attachments, responses, tasks, meetings, intelligence, and analytics. The equity path does not automatically fund a facility or equipment purchase.

## JV / Facility Partners

JV / Facility is a partnership-formation workflow for land, buildings, facilities, development, and operating relationships. It retains the organization, project or facility context, contribution expectations, ownership and economics, diligence stages, qualification state, and execution actions. A prospective partner is not a capital commitment until the record is explicitly advanced through the appropriate approval and commitment state.

## Equipment Finance and Equipment Rental

Equipment Finance covers identifiable productive assets such as robotics, CNC and fabrication equipment, material handling, and conveyance. The system separates acquisition, financing, qualification, lender or vendor context, and repayment terms. Equipment Rental is operating-use exposure and should not be counted as debt or funded equipment ownership.

## Grants & Incentives

Grants & Incentives covers source-attributed programs, opportunities, applications, awards, disbursements, and compliance. The workflow prevents an unqualified research record from being treated as an award or funded capital. Federal, Texas, and other program research must preserve source, status, eligibility, timing, and award-term uncertainty.

## Shared capital-stage boundaries

The shared foundation allows a project and capital opportunity to be associated without collapsing distinct stages. The system keeps the following states separate:

> Prospect ≠ Opportunity ≠ Commitment ≠ Funded Capital ≠ Contribution ≠ Cash Flow ≠ Return ≠ ROI.

The aggregate dashboard can compare these states, but it does not infer a later state from an earlier one. Modeled capital requirements are uses-of-capital context, not commitments or funded amounts.
