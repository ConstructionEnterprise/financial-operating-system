## Initial public-source research findings

The initial sourcing pass confirmed that Texas First Rentals publicly describes daily, weekly, and long-term rentals, delivery, emergency service, and a statewide set of listed locations. Tejas Equipment Rental publicly describes Texas coverage across the Rio Grande Valley, Central Texas, Fort Worth, and Houston, and it presents forklifts, earthmoving, and aerial equipment along with delivery and pickup language. United Rentals’ Texas location directory describes equipment and tool rentals plus category-specific locations throughout the state. Cat Rentals’ Texas page describes rental support from Texas dealers and a broad catalogue of construction, material-handling, power, and other equipment categories. These facts support research records only; availability, quotes, contact routing, qualification, and CE approval remain unrecorded until entered and reviewed.
