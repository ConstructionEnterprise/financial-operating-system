# CE/FF Consolidation Audit

**Reference date:** August 21, 2026  
**Scope:** User-facing navigation and long-form Internal Projects work surfaces

## Completed first consolidation

Internal Projects now has six primary command-ribbon destinations instead of nine. Cedarwood’s historical pro forma, Schedule + Debt workspace, and six-project historical comparison are project-economics subpanels rather than primary Internal Projects tabs. The former Cedarwood Schedule + Debt deep link was verified to open its mapped Project Economics subpanel after selected-project context loads.

The 36-month schedule defaults to a compact progress summary and an **Expand monthly editor** action. The 100-line WBS defaults to category, control-total, and reconciliation summaries with an **Expand 100-line WBS ledger** action. A completed debt calculation will likewise keep its 36-month debt ledger collapsed until explicitly expanded.
