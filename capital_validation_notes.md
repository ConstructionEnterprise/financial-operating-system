# Capital Operating System Validation Notes

Desktop review confirmed that the aggregate Capital Dashboard renders an executive capital plan with path-level target, committed, weighted-pipeline, and funding-gap figures. The dedicated **JV / Facility**, **Equipment Finance**, and **Grants & Incentives** routes render as separate operational workspaces with distinct add-opportunity and capital-target controls.

The empty-state presentation is readable and each specialized path preserves its distinct domain purpose. The added specialized detail forms appear after an opportunity is created, so no example counterparty data was introduced during validation.

Mobile review confirmed that capital targets, add-opportunity inputs, dashboard totals, and aggregate action cards stack without clipping. The compact icon rail preserves access to every capital path while the specialized Equipment Finance entry form remains readable and actionable.

The JV / Facility and Grants & Incentives mobile routes were also reviewed. Both retain readable path descriptions, full-width target controls, accessible entry forms, and clear empty states without horizontal clipping.

The expanded executive view now visibly separates total requirement, committed, funded, expected capital, remaining gap, and remaining expected gap. The JV workspace keeps the institutional visual language and presents a clean entry path before its deeper partnership-formation detail panel appears for a created opportunity.
