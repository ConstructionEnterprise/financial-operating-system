From capital formation to operating cash flow
The sequence is staged—not all funded at once.
The initial equity builds the technology layer; the facility and machinery are capitalized through separate vehicles.

05 / OUTPUT

Revenue

Production and customer delivery generate operating cash flow

Technology Company

Licenses and supports the platform; receives software, integration, and service fees.

Facility JV

Leases or provides the building under documented occupancy economics.

Equipment Lender

Receives debt service or lease payments against identifiable productive assets.
