Staged capitalization plan
Proposed execution sequence
Build the technology layer first, capitalize the physical site second, and add productive equipment as deployment and demand become visible.
01

Close technology equity

Raise the illustrative $1M / 10% round and fund the platform, team, and first deployment.

→
02

→
03

Execute platform, facility, equipment, data, cybersecurity, and governance documents.

→
04

Stage equipment debt

Order and finance machinery in tranches tied to validated deployment and demand milestones.

→
05

Commission and measure

Deploy the system, complete acceptance testing, and collect operating performance data.

→
06

Finance from proof

Use demonstrated performance to support additional customer, equipment, property, and technology capital.

Sequencing principleDo not ask the initial equity investor to finance the entire factory. Let technical proof unlock the next capital source.
