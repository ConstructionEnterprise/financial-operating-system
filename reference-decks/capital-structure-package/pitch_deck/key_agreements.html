Documentation layer
Key agreements make the separation real.
The financing structure only works if contracts clearly define ownership, access, payment flows, data rights, and remedies.
Four document families connect the technology company, facility JV, factory operator, and equipment lender.
01
Technology company + Factory OpCo

Platform license and services

License scope, fees, support, deployment, acceptance, confidentiality, transition assistance, and ownership of generalized improvements.

02
Facility JV + Factory OpCo

Facility lease or occupancy

Rent/occupancy fees, term and renewals, tenant improvements, taxes, insurance, maintenance, completion obligations, expansion rights, and exit mechanics.

03
Lender / lessor + operating borrower

Equipment finance and security

Eligible machinery, funding conditions, collateral, amortization, insurance, maintenance, covenants, permitted liens, acceptance testing, and repossession remedies.

04
All relevant entities

Data, cybersecurity, and governance

Allocation of customer and operating data, platform telemetry, security duties, incident response, related-party approvals, reserved matters, and deadlock procedures.

Control principleAgreements should let the factory use the platform and facility without allowing property ownership or equipment financing to create accidental ownership of core IP.
