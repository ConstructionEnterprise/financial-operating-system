Entity architecture
Keep the operating system separate from the factory shell.
Each entity owns or finances a different risk class, while the factory operator connects the platform, property, equipment, and customers.
Scalable IP owner

Technology Company

Builds, owns, licenses, and supports the automation platform.

  • Software platform
Physical site owner

Facility JV / PropCo

Owns or controls the land, building, and fixed site infrastructure.

  • Land and title
Revenue interface

Factory Operator

Runs production, serves customers, and turns capacity into operating revenue.

  • Production workforce
Asset financier

Equipment Finance Vehicle

Owns or holds security over identifiable productive machinery.

  • Robot arms
Ownership principle
The Technology Company licenses the platform into the factory; the Facility JV does not receive core IP merely because it provides the building.
License + services · Lease / occupancy · Equipment availability
