Ownership architecture
IP protection is a strategic advantage.
The Technology Company should own the scalable intelligence layer. The factory should receive the right to operate it—not the right to own it.
Technology Company

Owns the core platform

Source code, control logic, robotics intelligence, digital-twin architecture, data models, deployment tooling, documentation, and generalized platform improvements.

→
Factory OpCo

Receives a limited license

Enough rights to operate the deployed system, receive support, and use approved site-specific configurations within the agreed field of use.

→
Facility JV

Does not receive core IP

Property ownership or facility financing alone should not create ownership of software, robotics intelligence, or platform-level improvements.

Strategic consequence: the technology company can deploy the platform across additional facilities and customers without giving away the engine that makes the model scalable.
Recommended default for discussion; definitive ownership, licensing, data, and improvement rights require legal documentation.
