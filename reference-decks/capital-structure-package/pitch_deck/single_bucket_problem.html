Why separation matters
A factory is not one risk. It is several capital problems.

Single-capital-bucket model

01$1M equity attempts to fund software, land, construction, equipment, and working capital.
02One investor is asked to underwrite technology, real estate, and asset-financing risk together.
03Technology-company ownership may be diluted to capitalize a physical facility.
04Core IP can become entangled with property or operating partners.

Proposed separated model

01$1M equity funds platform, people, integration, and first deployment.
02A facility JV owns or finances land and building.
03Equipment lenders finance identifiable productive machinery.
04Core IP remains cleanly owned by the Technology Company.
The financing story becomes more capital-efficient because each provider funds the asset class it understands.
