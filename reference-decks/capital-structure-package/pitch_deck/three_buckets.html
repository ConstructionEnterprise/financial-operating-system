The proposed architecture
Three capital buckets. Three underwriting lenses.
Separate technology risk from real-estate risk and equipment-financing risk.
01 / EQUITY

$1M for 10%

Funds the technology and ops: platform, people, and first deployment.

02 / JOINT VENTURE

Land + building

Entity owns or controls the facility; funded by a land/building partner or property financing.

03 / DEBT

Productive equipment

Loans or leases finance robot arms, CNC, fabrication, and material handling.

Each provider funds the asset class it is best positioned to understand and underwrite.
