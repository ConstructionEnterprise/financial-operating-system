Closing thesis
The investment thesis in one sentence.
Use the right capital for the right asset—and preserve the technology company’s ability to scale.
Capital-efficient factory platform
The initial equity round builds the scalable technology layer; the facility JV capitalizes the physical site; and equipment debt funds productive machinery.

Technology equity

Funds platform, people, integration, productization, and first deployment.

Facility joint venture

Funds or owns land, building, infrastructure, and facility economics.

Equipment financing

Funds identifiable machinery whose productive use can support repayment.
