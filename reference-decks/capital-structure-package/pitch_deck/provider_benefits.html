Aligned incentives
Why each capital provider benefits
The structure gives each stakeholder exposure to the asset and risk class it is best positioned to understand.
01 / Technology investor

Owns the scalable layer

Underwrites platform growth

Receives exposure to the software, robotics, and factory-control platform with the ability to deploy beyond one facility.

02 / Land + building partner

Owns property economics

Underwrites real estate

Participates in land, building, and facility economics without requiring broad ownership of the technology company.

03 / Equipment lender

Finances productive assets

Underwrites collateral + cash flow

Provides loans or leases against identifiable machinery, with repayment supported by operating cash flow as utilization ramps.

04 / Factory operating company

Accesses capacity efficiently

Underwrites execution + demand

Uses the platform, facility, and equipment to serve customers while limiting unnecessary technology-company dilution.

Alignment principle: no single investor is asked to finance technology development, real estate, and equipment simultaneously.
